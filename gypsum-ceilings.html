Expert Gypsum Ceiling Design & Installation in Nairobi

Gypsum ceilings (also known as false ceilings or suspended plasterboard ceilings) are the gold standard for premium residential and commercial interior finishing in Kenya. At InteriorFinishes Nairobi, we deliver flawless gypsum ceiling systems that combine architectural beauty, lighting integration, thermal comfort, and acoustic performance.

Whether you want to transform a residential living room in Karen, design an office lobby in Westlands, or install minimalist false ceilings in a Kilimani apartment, our team of experienced artisans ensures precise framing, seamless board alignment, and a smooth paint finish.

Our Custom Styling & Design Options

  • Architectural Bulkheads: Multi-level stepped drops that visually demarcate functional zones (e.g., dining vs. lounge areas) in open-plan layouts.
  • Ambient Cove Lighting: Recessed pockets built into the ceiling structure that house LED strip lights to throw soft, indirect illumination across the room.
  • Flush Ceilings: Flat, seamless gypsum installations perfect for modern apartments, providing a clean surface that integrates downlights and HVAC vents.
  • Bespoke Cornices & Moldings: Decorative border finishes that hide the wall-ceiling joint, ranging from contemporary shadow lines to classic ornamental cornices.

Our Step-by-Step Installation Process

We strictly adhere to professional construction standards to prevent ceiling sagging, cracks, or joint failure over time. Here is how we execute each project:

  1. Site Survey & Laser Leveling: We use high-precision cross-line laser levels to establish a perfect level datum point across all walls.
  2. Galvanized Framing Setup: We install a robust, heavy-gauge steel framework consisting of perimeter wall angles, main carriers (furring channels), and suspensions anchored firmly to the concrete slab or roof trusses.
  3. Board Boarding: High-grade gypsum boards (9mm or 12mm thickness) are screwed onto the steel framing. We use moisture-resistant green boards for humid zones (bathrooms/kitchens) and fire-resistant pink boards where safety is critical.
  4. Joint Taping & Skimming: Joints are reinforced using fiber tape and filled with high-strength joint filler. The entire ceiling is then skimmed with gypsum plaster to create a uniform, smooth texture.
  5. Sanding, Priming & Painting: Once dry, the ceiling is sanded flat, sealed with an undercoat primer, and finished with premium matte emulsions to eliminate light reflections.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does gypsum ceiling installation take?

A: An average residential living room installation (30-40 square meters) with simple cove lighting bulkheads takes about 4 to 6 working days from framing to paint finish.

Q: Can gypsum ceilings crack over time?

A: Sinking or cracking only occurs if light-gauge steel channels are used, if suspensions are spaced too far apart, or if joints are poorly taped. We guarantee structural integrity by using premium-grade galvanized steel and fiber tape joint reinforcement.

Q: Are gypsum ceilings fireproof?

A: Gypsum boards contain chemically bound water which acts as a natural fire retardant. For commercial buildings, we use specialized fire-rated boards to comply with building regulations.
